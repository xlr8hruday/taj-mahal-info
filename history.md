# History of the Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal was commissioned in 1632 by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan as a mausoleum for his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal, who died during childbirth. Construction began in 1632 and was largely completed in 1648, with final touches continuing until 1653. The monument combines Persian, Islamic, and Indian architectural styles and is renowned for its white marble façade, intricate inlay work, and expansive gardens.

* Designed by a team of architects led by Ustad Ahmad Lahori.
* Built using over 20,000 artisans and craftsmen.
* The complex includes a main gateway, a garden, a mosque, a guest house, and the central mausoleum.

The Taj Mahal is recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is considered a masterpiece of world heritage.
